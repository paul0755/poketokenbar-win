Digimon dot sprites in this folder
==================================

Source: humulos.com (https://humulos.com/digimon/) - Vital Bracelet color dot sprites,
recreated by humulos and the Digitama Hatchery community or distributed via Wikimon (https://wikimon.net).
The only change made here: the two animation frames of each sprite were joined into one animated GIF
(<name>.gif), plus a single-frame copy (<name>-still.gif). Built by scripts/digimon.js.
